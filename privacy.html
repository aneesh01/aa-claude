Privacy policy

apartment-watch is a personal tool used only by its owner, Aneesh Aravind.

It requests a single Google permission, gmail.send, used only to send listing summary emails from the owner's Gmail account to the owner's own addresses. It does not read, store or share any Gmail data, and it collects no data from anyone else.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections.
